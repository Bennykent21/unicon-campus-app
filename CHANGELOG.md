# Changelog

Each official release's notes on GitHub come from its section here.
Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org).

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-14

UNICON is now open to students at every university, and each campus has its own space.

### New
- **Choose your university** when you sign up. You see posts, questions, trending topics and comments from your own campus only. Chat and following still work with students anywhere.
- **University not listed?** Tap "Ask us to add it" in the university picker. You can do this before you have an account.
- **School-only internships.** Most listings are open to every student; some are just for one university and say so.
- **Manage universities** (curators only): review requests and add universities from the Profile menu.

### Changed
- Already using UNICON? You'll be asked to confirm your university once. Your details are filled in for you.
- Edit Profile now opens with your details filled in instead of blank.
- Internship application emails use your own university.
- Internship listings are worded for students of any school.
